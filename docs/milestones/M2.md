### M2-01 · Tenant schema and ranks
- Tenant migration `0002_boards` adds projects, project_members, boards, board_access, board_stars, lists, cards, labels, card_labels, card_members, card_watchers — `test_tenant_schema.py::test_a_company_file_has_the_board_tables`.
- **Moving 200 cards creates no rank collisions and ranks stay under 32 characters**: random moves across three lists keep the expected order with unique ranks — `test_rank.py::test_moving_200_cards_at_random_keeps_order_and_unique_short_ranks`; 200 moves into the same gap rebalance the list and keep the order — `::test_moving_200_cards_into_the_same_gap_rebalances_and_keeps_order`; 500 cards added at the end stay ordered and short — `::test_adding_500_cards_at_the_end_keeps_ranks_short`.
- A key sorts strictly between its neighbours; open ends give a first and a last key; neighbours out of order or malformed are refused — `test_rank.py::test_a_key_between_two_neighbours_sorts_between_them`, `::test_open_ends_give_a_first_and_a_last_key`, `::test_neighbours_out_of_order_are_refused`, `::test_a_malformed_neighbour_is_refused`; a rebalance gives sorted, unique keys of at most 3 characters — `::test_a_spread_is_sorted_unique_and_short`.
- Lists move on their board the same way; moving after a card of another list, after itself, or a card that does not exist is refused — `::test_moving_a_list_changes_only_its_place_on_the_board`, `::test_moving_after_a_card_of_another_list_is_refused`, `::test_moving_a_card_that_does_not_exist_is_refused`.
- The schema refuses a malformed rank, an unknown project role, a card member who is not a person of the company, a blank name, and a label of another board, also when a card label is changed — `test_tenant_schema.py::test_a_malformed_rank_is_refused`, `::test_an_unknown_project_role_is_refused`, `::test_a_card_member_must_be_a_person_of_the_company`, `::test_a_blank_name_is_refused`, `::test_a_label_belongs_to_the_board_of_its_card`, `::test_a_card_label_cannot_be_changed_to_a_label_of_another_board`.
- Moving a card or a list to another board drops the labels of the old board; a move within the board keeps them — `::test_moving_a_card_to_another_board_drops_its_labels`, `::test_moving_a_list_to_another_board_drops_the_labels_of_its_cards`, `::test_moving_a_card_within_its_board_keeps_its_labels`.
- A deleted card's id is never given to a new card (events and share links name cards by id) — `::test_the_id_of_a_deleted_card_is_never_given_again`.
- Deleting a project removes its boards and everything on them, and leaves the people — `::test_deleting_a_project_removes_everything_on_its_boards`.
- Release 0.1.0 (tenant migration 0001) still opens a file migrated to 0002, so rolling back needs no restore (D21) — `::test_the_release_before_the_board_tables_still_opens_the_file`.
