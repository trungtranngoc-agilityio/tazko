### M2-01 · Tenant schema and ranks
- Tenant migration `0002_boards` adds projects, project_members, boards, board_access, board_stars, lists, cards, labels, card_labels, card_members, card_watchers — `test_tenant_schema.py::test_a_company_file_has_the_board_tables`.
- **Moving 200 cards creates no rank collisions and ranks stay under 32 characters**: random moves across three lists keep the expected order with unique ranks — `test_rank.py::test_moving_200_cards_at_random_keeps_order_and_unique_short_ranks`; 200 moves into the same gap rebalance the list and keep the order — `::test_moving_200_cards_into_the_same_gap_rebalances_and_keeps_order`; 500 cards added at the end stay ordered and short — `::test_adding_500_cards_at_the_end_keeps_ranks_short`.
- A key sorts strictly between its neighbours; open ends give a first and a last key; neighbours out of order or malformed are refused — `test_rank.py::test_a_key_between_two_neighbours_sorts_between_them`, `::test_open_ends_give_a_first_and_a_last_key`, `::test_neighbours_out_of_order_are_refused`, `::test_a_malformed_neighbour_is_refused`; a rebalance gives sorted, unique keys of at most 3 characters — `::test_a_spread_is_sorted_unique_and_short`.
- Lists move on their board the same way; moving after a card of another list, after itself, or a card that does not exist is refused — `::test_moving_a_list_changes_only_its_place_on_the_board`, `::test_moving_after_a_card_of_another_list_is_refused`, `::test_moving_a_card_that_does_not_exist_is_refused`.
- The schema refuses a malformed rank, an unknown project role, a card member who is not a person of the company, a blank name, and a label of another board, also when a card label is changed — `test_tenant_schema.py::test_a_malformed_rank_is_refused`, `::test_an_unknown_project_role_is_refused`, `::test_a_card_member_must_be_a_person_of_the_company`, `::test_a_blank_name_is_refused`, `::test_a_label_belongs_to_the_board_of_its_card`, `::test_a_card_label_cannot_be_changed_to_a_label_of_another_board`; a label never moves to another board, and a missing label is a foreign key error — `::test_a_label_cannot_move_to_another_board`, `::test_a_card_label_that_does_not_exist_is_a_foreign_key_error`.
- Moving a card or a list to another board drops the labels of the old board; a move within the board keeps them — `::test_moving_a_card_to_another_board_drops_its_labels`, `::test_moving_a_list_to_another_board_drops_the_labels_of_its_cards`, `::test_moving_a_card_within_its_board_keeps_its_labels`.
- A deleted project's, board's, list's, card's or label's id is never given to a new row (events and share links name them by id) — `::test_the_id_of_a_deleted_row_is_never_given_again`.
- Deleting a project removes its boards and everything on them, and leaves the people — `::test_deleting_a_project_removes_everything_on_its_boards`.
- The release before 0002 (tenant migration 0001 only) still reads and writes a file migrated to 0002, so rolling back needs no restore (D21) — `::test_the_release_before_the_board_tables_still_opens_the_file`.

### M2-02 · Projects and permissions
Part 1 (projects and members). Parts 2 to 4 add their lines.
- The permission matrix: every action for company owners in and out of the project, company members out of it, project owners, members and observers, with the switches and an archived project — unit `test_project_access.py::test_permission_matrix` (77 cells), `::test_members_invite_when_the_project_allows_it`, `::test_members_cannot_create_boards_when_the_project_says_so`, `::test_an_archived_project_allows_only_reads_and_its_management`.
- **A company member who is not an owner is refused when creating a project** — integration `test_projects.py::test_a_company_member_who_is_not_an_owner_cannot_create_a_project`; a company owner creates one with its first owner, who must be in the company — `::test_a_company_owner_creates_a_project_with_its_first_owner`, `::test_the_first_owner_must_be_in_the_company`, `::test_a_project_needs_a_valid_name_and_fields`.
- **A company member who is not in a project cannot see it** (404 on the project, its members and writes) — `::test_a_company_member_outside_the_project_cannot_see_it`; people see only their own projects; company owners see every project on Manage projects, nobody else does — `::test_people_see_only_the_projects_they_belong_to`, `::test_company_owners_see_every_project_on_the_manage_view`, `::test_the_manage_view_is_for_company_owners_only`.
- **A project observer cannot edit** — `::test_an_observer_cannot_change_anything`.
- **A company owner can archive and delete a project and add owners**, but changes its other settings only once in it; a project owner cannot archive or delete — `::test_an_archived_project_can_be_read_but_not_changed_until_restored`, `::test_a_deleted_project_is_gone_for_everyone`, `::test_a_company_owner_manages_project_owners_but_not_plain_members`, `::test_a_company_owner_cannot_change_the_settings_of_a_project_they_are_not_in`, `::test_a_project_owner_cannot_archive_or_delete_the_project`. Boards stay closed to them until they are added: part 2.
- A company owner joining a project is audit-logged however they join (adding themselves, being added, creating it as owner) — `::test_a_company_owner_adds_themselves_and_it_is_audit_logged`, `::test_a_company_owner_joining_a_project_is_audit_logged_however_they_join`.
- Someone outside the project changes nothing when they try (404, no row and no event written, not even a person copy) — `::test_someone_outside_the_project_changes_nothing_when_they_try`; a member with no copy in the company file gets one, with its event — `::test_adding_someone_with_no_copy_in_the_company_file_makes_it_with_an_event`.
- People removed from the company leave its projects (D6), so no stale owner satisfies the last-owner check and a re-invite brings back no role — `::test_people_removed_from_the_company_leave_its_projects`.
- Anyone may leave a project, except its last owner; archiving twice changes nothing — `::test_a_member_can_leave_a_project_but_the_last_owner_cannot`, `::test_archiving_an_archived_project_changes_nothing`.
- Project owners change the settings (aging 3, 7, 14 or 30 days) — `::test_a_project_owner_changes_the_settings`, `::test_aging_days_are_one_of_the_offered_choices`, `::test_null_clears_the_colour_but_is_not_a_name`.
- Members: add, list, change, conflicts, people outside the company, the "members can invite" switch — `::test_a_project_owner_adds_lists_and_changes_members`, `::test_a_person_outside_the_company_cannot_be_added`, `::test_adding_someone_already_in_the_project_is_a_conflict`, `::test_a_member_adds_people_only_when_the_project_allows_it`, `::test_giving_someone_the_role_they_have_changes_nothing`, `::test_changing_someone_who_is_not_in_the_project_is_404`.

- D6: removing a member takes them off assignees, watchers and restricted boards; their cards stay; one event is written — `::test_removing_a_member_unassigns_them_and_ends_their_board_access`.

- **A project observer cannot edit** boards; members cannot create boards when the project says so — `::test_an_observer_cannot_create_a_board`, `::test_members_cannot_create_boards_when_the_project_says_so`, `::test_a_member_renames_and_closes_a_board_and_an_observer_cannot`.
- **A company owner cannot open a project's boards until added** (403 `NOT_IN_PROJECT`), and can once they add themselves — `::test_a_company_owner_opens_boards_only_once_added_to_the_project`; a company member outside the project finds no boards (404); a board answers only under its own project — `::test_a_company_member_outside_the_project_finds_no_boards`, `::test_a_board_is_found_only_under_its_own_project`.

- An open board takes no list (422) and an unchanged one writes no event; project owners get every restricted list in one listing — `::test_an_open_board_takes_no_list_and_an_unchanged_one_writes_no_event`, `::test_project_owners_get_every_restricted_list_in_one_listing`.
