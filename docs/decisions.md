| D22 | How does the server check Google's ID token, and where does the sign-in state live? | PyJWT with `cryptography` (MIT; Apache-2.0 or BSD) verifies the RS256 signature against Google's published keys, plus audience, issuer, expiry and nonce; the keys are cached for an hour and refetched at most once a minute on an unknown key id. | D25 | M1-05: where do invites live, how does an invited person reach the company file, and how is Origin enforced? | A new `invites` module (service + repository); `auth` calls it at sign-in. Inviting a member (409 `ALREADY_MEMBER`), into a company that is not active (409 `COMPANY_NOT_ACTIVE`), twice (409 `INVITE_PENDING`), or revoking a used or revoked invite (409 `INVITE_USED`, `INVITE_REVOKED`) is refused. Accepting is part of the one sign-in transaction in control.db: create the person (only with a usable invite), mark each usable invite used with a guarded UPDATE, add the membership (an existing role never changes), audit `invite.accepted`. The person's copy in the company file is made the first time they open the company (`open_company`), which also repairs a crash between the two writes of `add_member`; this is the one case where a GET writes, and it only repairs the copy. | D27 | M1-06 company settings API: shape and rules | `GET /api/orgs` (switcher, suspended companies listed with their status, per Q2). The "Email the invitation" option waits for email (M6). `create_company` needs a super admin, `add_member` an owner or super admin, checked in the service. | M1-06, M1-07 | proposed |
